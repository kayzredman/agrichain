# Design laws

These are the constraints. A feature that breaks one of them repeats a failure already described on the episode. Where a law lands on a lot is drawn in the [blueprint](blueprint.md#path-of-a-lot).

| Law | From the episode | What agriChain does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Custody, not a coin | The ledger is chain of custody. The money story made people skip the journey. | Events are signed handoffs of a lot. No token sits underneath the commodity. |
| Value chain, not a pipe | Supply is movement. Value is what the origin country keeps before export. | Every process event records the country that captured that step. |
| Translucent, not glass | Banks learned that a public window also briefs the attacker. | The record is complete. The default view is narrow. A right opens the rest. |
| Origin cannot be rewritten | The processing country currently wins the label. | Harvest fixes biological origin. Later events may add a process country. They never replace the first. |
| Implausible before criminal | Land cannot yield five times itself. A normal flow is not audited. | Yield ceilings and mass balance raise alerts. Investigators are not the daily path. |
| The crime is off the ledger | OTC names, fake papers, and shell directors are not chain data. | Store hashes and lot paths. Hand a package to an investigation graph when an alert fires. |
| Open, with a reason for the middle | One company, or one boss over many farmers, cannot carry it. Farmers and governments understand. Brokers resist. | Any certified actor can write events they witnessed. Each role gets a concrete benefit for doing it. |
| Capital, not charity | Donors pulled back. Borrowing in dollars to satisfy a lender is not development. | Adoption is paid by retained processing value, faster claims, and dispute evidence. |

## AI

She expects a turn away from AI for its own sake. People are still needed to judge an unexpected output. Use models to rank alerts. A model does not invent a custody event or a biological origin.
