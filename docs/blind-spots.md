# Blind spots

The honest path of a lot:

`Grow → Gather → Make → Move → Claim → Sell`

Parcel and season, then many farms into one lot, then process and bag, then ship and port, then origin, grade, and date, then the over-the-counter trade and retail.

Each gap below is one she described. The third column is the record agriChain has to keep if that gap is to become visible.

| Gap | What goes wrong | Record that exposes it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Aggregation | Many growers become one factory intake. Nobody is named in between. | A custodian on every merge, with quantities in and out. |
| Factory balance | More leaves than arrived. The adulterant is the difference. | Inputs, additives, loss, and outputs on the same process event. |
| Legal origin | The processor's country becomes the origin on the label. | Biological origin frozen at harvest. Processing is a later event. |
| The voyage | The ship can stop. Crew may be coerced. Documents can be swapped. | Declared ports versus actual calls. Document hashes, not the paper alone. |
| OTC trade | Days of phone brokerage and ICC papers never hit a ledger. | A trade reference off the ledger, linked by hash. People stay in the investigation file. |
| Expiry and QR | Stale goods re-enter with a new code and a new date. | Expiry bound to the lot at packing. Relabel is its own signed event. |
| Shell network | A Singapore director, a Cayman account, a factory in Italy. | Export the lot path for a graph tool when an alert fires. Do not store that network as gossip on the chain. |
